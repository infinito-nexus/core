# Where to get music you may use

Licences change and differ per track: read the licence page for the exact track before using it, and record what you read in `audio/SOURCE.md` (title, artist, URL, licence, date, bars used, credit line). Never commit the downloaded track to a public repository unless its licence allows redistributing the file itself (CC BY does, with credit; most "free for projects" libraries don't).

| Source | Licence in short | Credit | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org) | per track: pick Creative Commons **BY** (or CC0) | as the licence says | avoid NC (non-commercial) and ND for a product film |
| [ccMixter](https://ccmixter.org) | Creative Commons per track | required for BY | same: BY or CC0 only for commercial use |
| [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/music/) (Kevin MacLeod) | CC BY 4.0, or a paid licence without credit | required under CC BY | credit line in the description or end card |
| [Free Stock Music](https://www.free-stock-music.com) | per track, many CC BY 4.0 | usually required | check each track's page |
| [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/) | Pixabay Content License | not required | the file itself may not be redistributed; some tracks carry Content ID |
| [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/) | Mixkit Stock Music Free License | not required | not for redistribution on its own; read the licence for broadcast use |
| [YouTube Audio Library](https://studio.youtube.com) | free for use in videos | some tracks require it | licence is written for YouTube uploads; read it for other platforms |
| [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io) | free tier with a credit, paid without | free tier: required | free tier needs an account; limits per month |
| Paid libraries (Artlist, Musicbed, Epidemic Sound) | subscription licences | no | covers commercial use and social ads while the subscription covers the publication |

Good launch tracks: 110–135 BPM, a steady pulse, 8–16 bars of build, a clear drop, a quieter breakdown, no vocals if there will be a voiceover.
